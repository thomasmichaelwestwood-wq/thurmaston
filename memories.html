Memories of Thurmaston

Old photographs, personal stories and shared history — an archive built by the people who lived it.

A brief history

The historic heart of the village stands on Melton Road, which follows the route of the old Fosse Way — the Roman road running from Exeter to Lincoln. Thurmaston St Michael was formed as an ecclesiastical parish in 1794, combining a chapelry in Belgrave with the South Thurmaston chapelry.

The village sits on the eastern bank of the River Soar, bordered by Watermead Country Park to the west, Syston to the north, and Barkby and Barkby Thorpe to the east. Two marinas on the River Soar reflect the village's long relationship with the water.

This is a brief starting point, not a complete history — if you have corrections, sources, or a longer account to contribute, we'd love to include it.
