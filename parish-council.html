Thurmaston Parish Council

Your local tier of government, responsible for community buildings, open spaces, allotments, play areas and more.

This is the official council?

Thurmaston Parish Council runs its own website with full statutory information (agendas, minutes, finance, councillor register of interests) at thurmastonpc.gov.uk ↗. This page is a village-site summary that links out to it — for anything formal or time-sensitive, the council's own site is the authoritative source.

Meetings, agendas & minutes

Full Council usually meets monthly, with additional committee meetings as needed. Dates, agendas and minutes are published on the official council website at least three clear days before each meeting, in line with the Public Bodies (Admission to Meetings) Act 1960.

  • Full Council Monthly — see the official site for the current schedule and agenda.
  • Planning Committee Meets as needed to review local planning applications.
  • Finance & General Purposes Oversees budget, precept and council administration.

View agendas & minutes on the official site ↗

What the Parish Council does

Community buildings & spaces

Looks after open spaces, allotments, play areas and community facilities including Elizabeth Park Sports & Community Centre.

Planning consultation

Reviews and comments on local planning applications on behalf of residents, feeding into Charnwood Borough Council's decisions.

Grants & local projects

Supports local groups and projects through small grants and by championing village initiatives.

Precept & finance

Sets the parish precept (the parish share of council tax) and publishes its accounts each year for public scrutiny.
