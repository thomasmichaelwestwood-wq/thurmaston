Public Buildings & Places

The community centres, places of worship, library, schools and green spaces that make up the village.

Photo: Elizabeth Park Sports & Community Centre

Elizabeth Park Sports & Community Centre

Checkland Road, Thurmaston, Leicester LE4 8FN

The village's main community hub — home to the Parish Council office, badminton courts, a state-of-the-art 3G football pitch, and a popular venue for weddings and functions.

Parish Council office Sports facilities Wedding venue Function hire

St Michael's Church

The parish church at the heart of Thurmaston St Michael, an ecclesiastical parish formed in 1794 from a chapelry in Belgrave combined with the South Thurmaston chapelry.

Church of England Historic parish, est. 1794
Photo: St Michael's Church
Photo: Thurmaston Library

Thurmaston Library

Leicestershire County Council library branch serving the village — books, computer access, and children's activities.

Opening hours and services: confirm current details with Leicestershire County Council's library service before visiting.

Watermead Country Park

A country park and nature reserve straddling the River Soar on the village's western edge, facing onto Birstall. A popular spot for walking, cycling and birdwatching, with lakes formed from former gravel workings.

Nature reserve Walking & cycling River Soar
