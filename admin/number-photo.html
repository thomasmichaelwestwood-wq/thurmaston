Number a group photo

Choose a photo, then click on each person (in whatever order you like) to drop a numbered marker. Click a marker again to remove it — the rest renumber automatically so they always run 1, 2, 3… with no gaps. When you're done, copy the JSON below and paste it into that photo's "Numbered people (JSON)" field in the admin.

Nothing is uploaded anywhere — this only runs in your browser. Upload the photo itself via the normal Photos collection as usual, either before or after numbering it here.

Click anywhere on the photo to add the next number. Click a marker to remove it.
